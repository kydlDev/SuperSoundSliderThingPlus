How do the sliders work?

Press F6 and open the Game menu sounds tab (Master, Music, Sound effects) or the Special adjustments tab (portal, shield generator, weather, wolf pup, chicken love). The same sliders are in the F1 config menu. All sliders are mute levels: 0% is full loudness and 100% is muted. Master, Music and Sound effects follow the game's own volume settings both ways; the weather, wolf pup, shield generator, chicken love and portal sliders only change those sounds.
